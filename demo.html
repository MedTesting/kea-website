Book a demo.

Thirty minutes, on a call, with a log in front of us. Fill in the form and we reply by email with times.

What happens on the call

  1. You bring a log, or we use a public one. A CSV or XES export from the system in question is ideal; a sample of a few thousand cases is enough. If you cannot share data yet, we work on the traffic-fines log from the video.
  2. We import it live. Map the three columns, let the clustering run, and open the map.
  3. We look at the first loop or wait together. Which cases, how many, how long, and what they have in common. You keep the History export from the session.

Before you book

Does my data leave my machine during the demo?

If you share a sample with us, it is loaded into Kea on the machine we present from and deleted afterwards. If we screen-share while you drive Kea on your own machine, nothing leaves it at all. How the app handles data.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Only the export. Kea needs a case identifier, an activity name and a timestamp per row; any other columns come along as attributes.

Is this a sales call?

It is a working session on a log. If Kea fits, we talk about a licence afterwards. How licensing works.
