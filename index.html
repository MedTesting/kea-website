CLUSTERS

Group the cases by how they actually ran.

Kea clusters cases with similar event patterns and draws each cluster as bands: one column per step, width by frequency. C1 and C2 are the two ways an order is meant to go. C3 is 1,276 orders that went through Credit check at least twice.

Drag K to split or merge clusters. The recommended K is computed when the log is imported.

WHAT ELSE TO KNOW

Built for data you are not allowed to upload.

Runs on your computer

A desktop app for macOS and Windows. Your data never leaves your machine, and the app does not need an internet connection.

Your existing logs

CSV or XES in, mapped in a four-step import. Logs with millions of events stream in without exhausting memory.

Every step on record

Each action lands in a History tree you can step back through. Export it as a file and a colleague sees exactly the path you took.

AI, optional and local

Cluster explanations and plain-English filtering run on a local Ollama model, or not at all. The core analysis needs none of it.
